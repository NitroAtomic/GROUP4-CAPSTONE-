Exercise 3: Phishing Email Analyzer

Examine three suspicious emails below. Identify which ones are phishing or vishing attempts and understand why.

Analyze the Emails

Read each email below carefully. Decide if each one is legitimate or a phishing/vishing attempt. Look for red flags like suspicious sender addresses, urgent language, and requests for sensitive information.

From: support@paypa1.com

Subject: URGENT: Verify Your Account Immediately

Date: Today at 2:45 PM

Dear Valued Customer,

We have detected suspicious activity on your PayPal account.

Click the link below to verify your identity immediately or your account will be suspended.

Click here to verify your account

PayPal Security Team

Is this email legitimate or phishing?

From: hr@ourcompany.com

Subject: Updated Employee Handbook

Date: Today at 9:30 AM

Hi Team,

We've updated our employee handbook with new policies.

Please review the attached document and let us know if you have any questions.

You can download it from our secure portal using your company login credentials.

Best regards,
Human Resources Department

Is this email legitimate or phishing?
