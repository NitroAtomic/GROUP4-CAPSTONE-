Have you ever received a sweet letter or a kind offer that seemed too good to be true? Phishing is one of the most common online tricks, and it is like theft but happens digitally. Phishing does use technology flaws, but it mostly targets your trust. Think of it like someone pretending to be a suitor or a trusted person sending you a beautiful letter or a wonderful offer that looks so real and sincere. They seem so caring and honest, just like someone asking for your heart or your trust, so you happily agree and let them in. But hidden inside is something dangerous, just like powder that looks harmless at first but brings harm once you accept it. Online phishing works the exact same way. Attackers send emails, fake websites, texts, and calls that look perfectly real and kind. They trick you into agreeing and sharing your private information willingly, and once you do, that is when your hardship begins. It slowly destroys your reputation, your integrity, and even the trust you have in yourself. To stay safe, always research and verify their credentials before you say yes to anyone, even if they seem familiar. Never agree or decide on something without knowing their true character, because you may know them on the surface but not who they really are. Most of all, remember that your future and your future family’s well-being depend on the choices you make, so take your time, confirm everything first, and never rush into trust.
